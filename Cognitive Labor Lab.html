Cognitive Labor Lab

A Zollman-style network model of scientists choosing between an old method and a new one.

Communication network

Switching networks keeps the same scientists and starting beliefs.

The old method succeeds 50% of the time, and everyone knows it.

The model. A scientist uses the new method only if their credence that it beats the old one is above 0.5. Each round, new-method users run their trials. Everyone then updates by Bayes' rule on their own results and their neighbors' results.

Scientists on the old method learn nothing new about the new one. If everyone drops below 0.5, the community stops testing it for good.

Hypotheses: the new method succeeds at 50% + ε or 50% − ε. The community settles when everyone is above 0.99 or everyone is at or below 0.5.
